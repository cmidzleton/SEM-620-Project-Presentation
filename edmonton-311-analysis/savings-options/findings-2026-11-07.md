# Higher-savings options, Edmonton 311 (Nov 7, 2026)
Source data: 311 Requests q7ua-agfg (2023-Nov 7 2026), six-part CSV in project files. Loaded in DuckDB.

Wasted referral = status detail in {duplicate x3, File Already under Investigation, 311 Redirecting, Vehicle Gone Upon Arrival, Does Not Meet Bylaw Offence Standards, Assessed-No Action Required, Unable to Contact Complainant, Location Not Found, Non-City Service or Location, Reported same day, Work per schedule/route x3, Unable to Locate Animal}.
- 2025 phone referrals 191,521 -> 26,885 wasted (14.0%); app 169,088 -> 54,936 (32.5%). 2026 YTD: phone 29,533, app 70,006.
- Bounces alone (Route Check target) = 4,995 in 2025.
- Top 10 phone codes = 47% of wasted; top 20 = 61%. Per-code waste rate 2024 vs 2025 corr 0.74 (402 codes, n>=30).
- Parking "Vehicle Gone Upon Arrival" 19,975 (2025): 9.7% when closed <1h vs 44.2% at 3-12h.
- Duplicates (all channels): 27.9K 2023, 30.8K 2024, 37.1K 2025, 49.6K 2026 YTD. Same code+neighbourhood within 3 days: 59.7% of dups vs 42.3% of non-dups (weak; needs address).
- Phone Full Service 2025: 473,564; lookup-type (info/trip plan/schedule/hours/balance/general/status) 348,631. Top: Trip Plan 33.9K, Facility hours 19.1K, Bus info 18.2K, Tax balance 13.0K.

Cost anchors: Edmonton $14.66M 311 budget / 900K = $16.29 per contact (team proposal, budget.edmonton.ca); Toronto $11-16/call, app ~$0.10 (Global News Jan 19 2024); labour 70-80% of call centre cost (Governing 2012); Minneapolis $4.50 call vs $0.50 web (GovTech 2010); Edmonton closed 311 Sundays Mar 2025, 6.5% of calls (Global News Feb 12 2025). No sourced cost per field visit: $25-60 is an assumption.
